# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Unopened items can be refunded within 14 days of delivery. Opened items qualify for store credit only, unless they are defective.

## Digital goods
Digital downloads are refundable only while the download link has not been used. Once the link is used, the purchase is final.

## Defective items
Defective items can be returned for a full refund or replacement within 90 days. We cover return shipping for defective items.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Store credit is issued immediately.
