# Support

## Hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00. Chat replies within 5 minutes during opening hours.

## Channels
You can reach support by chat, by email at support@example.com, or by phone. Phone support is for order and warranty issues only.

## Escalation
If an issue is not resolved within 2 business days it is escalated to a senior agent who will call you back.
