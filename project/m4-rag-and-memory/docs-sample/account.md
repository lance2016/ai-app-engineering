# Account

## Password reset
Reset your password from the login page using the "forgot password" link. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication can be enabled under Security settings and works with any authenticator app.

## Locked accounts
An account is locked after five failed login attempts. Contact support to unlock it; the lock lifts automatically after 24 hours.

## Deleting your account
You can delete your account from Privacy settings. Deletion removes personal data within 30 days and is irreversible.
