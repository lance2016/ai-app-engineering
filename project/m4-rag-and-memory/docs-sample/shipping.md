# Shipping

## Domestic delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders over 50. Orders under 50 pay a flat 6.

## Express delivery
Express delivery arrives the next business day for orders placed before 14:00. Express costs 15 regardless of order size.

## Same-day dispatch
Orders placed before the 14:00 cutoff are dispatched the same day. Orders after the cutoff ship the next business day.

## International
International shipping adds 3 to 7 business days. Customs duties are paid by the recipient on delivery.
