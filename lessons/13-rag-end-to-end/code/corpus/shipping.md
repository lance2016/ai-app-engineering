# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the mainland. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an extra 20.

Orders placed before 14:00 on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed later are dispatched the next business day.

We do not ship to PO boxes. International shipping is available to 30 countries; customs duties are paid by the recipient.

Tracking numbers are emailed once the parcel leaves the warehouse. If a parcel shows as delivered but is missing, contact support within 3 days so we can open a carrier investigation.
