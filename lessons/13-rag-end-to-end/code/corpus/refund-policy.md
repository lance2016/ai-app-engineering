# Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of delivery for unopened items. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item is received.

Opened items can be exchanged but not refunded, except when the item arrived damaged. Damaged items must be reported with photos within 48 hours of delivery.

Digital products are not refundable once the download link has been used. If the link was never used, a refund can be requested within 7 days of purchase.

Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by our error, such as a wrong or defective item.
