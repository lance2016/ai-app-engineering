# Warranty

All hardware products carry a 24-month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accessories such as cables and cases carry a 6-month warranty.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or unauthorized repairs. Opening the device voids the warranty unless done by an authorized service center.

To make a claim, provide the order number and a description of the fault. Repairs take 7 to 10 business days; a replacement is offered if a repair is not possible.

Warranty repairs are free of charge, including return shipping to the customer.
