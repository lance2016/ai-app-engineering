# Account and privacy

You can delete your account from the settings page. Deletion removes personal data within 30 days, except invoices, which we must keep for 7 years for tax reasons.

Two-factor authentication can be enabled with any TOTP app. Recovery codes are shown once; store them somewhere safe.

We never ask for your password by email or phone. Support staff can see your order history but not your payment card number.

Marketing emails can be turned off with one click at the bottom of any email. Transactional emails such as order confirmations cannot be turned off.
